# G3-Rover-Chassis-Design

## Project Overview
Mechanical design and development of a rover chassis using SolidWorks, structural analysis, and fabrication.
This project involved the design and fabrication of a rover capable of
transporting a sample across a specified distance.

I served as the Chassis Lead and was responsible for the design and
development of the rover's structural frame.

## My Role

As Chassis Lead, I worked on:

- Chassis geometry and layout
- Structural support
- Corner reinforcement
- Weight distribution
- Component mounting
- Integration with other rover systems

## Tools & Technologies

- SolidWorks
- SolidWorks Simulation / FEA
- 3D Printing
- Aluminum fabrication
